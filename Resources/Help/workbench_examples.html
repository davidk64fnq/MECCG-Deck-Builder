Modular Workbench: Drafting Specific Cards

Work in Progress: The Modular Deck Assembly Workbench (Deck > Assemble from Components... [Ctrl+M]) is still under active development. Rather than documenting every workbench control in full, this page provides reproducible examples showing how to steer its priority-based pipeline toward specific card combinations.
Jump to Example: How Targeting Works • Example 1: Army of the Dead (+ Paths of the Dead) • Example 2: Hobbits in Recruits vs. Starting Company

How to Coax Specific Cards Out of the Workbench

The workbench does not ask for exact card titles on character or faction rows. Instead, each tab filters and ranks eligible cards based on what earlier steps drafted into the deck:

  • Regions & Sites (Step 1): Determines which regions, adventure sites, and Havens are present in Sites.
  • Starting Company (Step 2) & Wizards & Recruits (Step 3): Combine Skill Focus, Species / Race, Mind, and DI thresholds to narrow a row down to a specific character (or small group), while ranking candidates by home location:
    1. Tier 1: Characters tied to the drafted regions/sites (or Havens inside those regions).
    2. Tier 2: Characters tied to outside Havens (all 4 Hero Havens are always included in Sites).
    3. Tier 3: Any remaining matching character—provided their Site Requirement tag (if any) is satisfied in Sites for Wizards & Recruits.
  • Factions (Step 4): Only considers factions playable at drafted sites/Havens whose Specific character and Prerequisite chain are all legal, then ranks Faction Bonus and Specific character matches at Tier 1.

Example 1: Drafting Army of the Dead (and Paths of the Dead)

Army of the Dead requires three separate pieces across three different parts of Middle-earth: Vale of Erech (Lamedon), Paths of the Dead (playable at Dunharrow in Rohan), and Aragorn II (whose home site is Bree in Arthedain).

Reproducible Workbench Settings

  1. Catalog Menu: Ensure The Wizards (METW) is checked.
  2. Regions & Sites tab:
    • Seed Region: Lamedon
    • Region Count: 5
    • Min / Max Adventure Sites: 4 / 12
    • Region Terrains: Check Border-land and Free-domain only (uncheck all others).
    • Site Holds: Check Free-hold and Border-hold only (uncheck all others).
  3. Starting Company tab: Configure Row 1 only (leave Rows 2–5 blank):
    • Skill Focus: Ranger (or Diplomat)
    • Species / Race: Dúnadan
    • Mind: >=9
  4. Wizards & Recruits tab: Leave blank (or configure any additional recruits you want).
  5. Factions tab: Keep defaults (1 in Play Deck / Resources, 1 in Sideboard).
  6. Return to General Settings and click Run Pipeline.

What Happens Under the Hood

  • Why Region Count = 5 instead of 4: With only Border-land and Free-domain checked, Belfalas forms a triangle with Lamedon and Lebennin. At Region Count = 4, expanding from Lamedon picks {Lamedon, Belfalas, Lebennin, Anórien} 87.5% of the time and only reaches Rohan 12.5% of the time. Raising Region Count to 5 guarantees that both Lamedon (Vale of Erech) and Rohan (Dunharrow) are drafted every time.
  • Out-of-region Aragorn II via Tier 3: Aragorn II is the only Dúnadan with Mind >= 9. Because his home site (Bree in Arthedain) is outside the southern cluster, he drafts via Tier 3 into Pool.
  • Automatic Prerequisite Drafting: In Factions, Army of the Dead sees Aragorn II (Specific), Vale of Erech (Site Requirement), and Dunharrow (Paths of the Dead's Site Requirement). Because it matches a drafted character via Specific, it promotes to Tier 1 and is drafted into Resources along with Paths of the Dead automatically.

Example 2: Drafting a Hobbit into Recruits vs. Starting Company

Under MECCG rules, Hobbits can begin in your Starting Company (Pool) at Rivendell regardless of whether Bag End is in your Site deck. During the game, however, Hobbits may only be brought into play at Bag End (never at a Haven).

To prevent dead cards, the workbench enforces each character's Site Requirement tag (Bag End on Hobbits) differently across the two tabs:

  • Starting Company (Pool): Ignores Site Requirement. A Hobbit row drafts cleanly into Pool via Tier 3 even when The Shire / Bag End is not in Sites.
  • Wizards & Recruits (Resources / Sideboard): Requires Site Requirement to be present in Sites. A Hobbit will only draft into Resources or Sideboard if Bag End is already in the drafted Site deck.

Reproducible Settings to Draft a Hobbit into Recruits

  1. Regions & Sites tab:
    • Seed Region: The Shire
    • Region Count: 4 (or higher)
    • Region Terrains: Ensure Free-domain is checked (The Shire is a Free-domain).
    • Site Holds: Ensure Free-hold is checked (Bag End is a Free-hold).
  2. Wizards & Recruits tab: On any row under Play Deck Characters (Resources) or Sideboard Characters, set:
    • Species / Race: Hobbit
    • Optional narrowing: e.g., Skill Focus = Scout and Mind = >=5 to target Frodo or Bilbo, or leave blank for any Hobbit.
  3. Return to General Settings and click Run Pipeline.

Because The Shire puts Bag End into Sites, the Hobbit satisfies Tier 1 and is drafted into Resources (or Sideboard). If you run the same row with Bag End excluded (for example, Seed Region = Lamedon or Free-hold unchecked), Wizards & Recruits will block the draft and report that the character's Site Requirement is not met in Sites.
