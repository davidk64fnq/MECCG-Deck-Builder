Allies (Resources & Company Retinue)

Allies are unique Resource entities that accompany a company like characters, but do not count toward company size limits, cannot bear items, and are controlled without Direct Influence. They provide valuable Marshalling Points (MPs), tactical movement acceleration, strike evasion, or alternative faction recruitment. Because allies enter play at specific geographic locations during the site phase, taxonomy tagging focuses on their staging destinations and tactical mechanisms.

1. Tagging Approach & Scope Discipline

Site Requirement

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Resource (Ally)

Records mandatory staging locations required to bring the ally into play. Semicolon lists define alternative staging options. Dynamic predicates (such as Region=Northern Rhovanion) are used when an ally can enter play at any site within a territory.

Representative Examples:
  • Old Forest → Staging site for Tom Bombadil
  • Wellinghall → Staging site for Treebeard
  • Bag End; Bree → Alternative staging sites for Bill the Pony
  • Dunharrow; Edoras → Alternative staging sites for Shadowfax
  • Region=Northern Rhovanion → Dynamic regional predicate for Roäc the Raven (METD)

Region Requirement

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Resource (Ally)

Assigned strictly when an ally's recruitment is geographically tied to an entire regional territory rather than a single fixed site. Enables Geographic Theater generation rules to draft native regional allies automatically.

Representative Example:
  • Northern Rhovanion → Regional recruitment boundary for Roäc the Raven (METD)

Role

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Resource (Ally)

Classifies the primary tactical game mechanism provided by the ally to the accompanying company.

Representative Examples:
  • Movement → Extra movement phases, emergency Haven relocation, or flight (Bill the Pony, Gwaihir, Shadowfax)
  • Protection → Strike immunity against automatic attacks, attack evasion, or hazard cancellation (Treebeard, Goldberry, Tom Bombadil)
  • Recruitment → Alternative faction recruitment mechanisms bypassing influence checks (Roäc the Raven)
What is Intentionally OMITTED on Allies:
  • No Home Attribute: Allies are resource cards, not characters. They possess no printed starting site or healing haven. Staging sites belong strictly under Site Requirement.
  • No Role: Combat on Defensive Allies: Ents and nature spirits possess high defensive prowess and strike immunity, but do not grant combat prowess bonuses or extra strikes to other characters. Classifying them as Role: Combat would pollute combat trick filters.
  • No Hard Dependencies for Soft Triggers (Gollum): Although Gollum can discard The One Ring, he is an autonomous defensive ally. Under the Primary Usability Rule, he is never tagged with Specific: The One Ring, ensuring open utility rules can draft him safely.
  • No Post-Entry Roaming Limits: Geographic discard boundaries (such as Ents or Tom Bombadil discarding when moving outside their home lands) are omitted from Region Requirement to prevent false drafting into decks that lack their actual staging sites.

2. Intended Procedural Drafting Patterns

Pattern A: The Universal Ally & Auto-Staging Engine

Drafts any random ally from your active expansions (including fixed-stronghold allies and regional enablers like Roäc the Raven), automatically guaranteeing that a legal destination site is added to the site deck without branching logic or fallbacks: 

[
  {
    "Name": "Universal Company Ally",
    "SaveAs": "RetinueAlly",
    "Section": "Resources",
    "Count": 1,
    "Attributes": {
      "Primary": "Resource",
      "Secondary": "Ally"
    }
  },
  {
    "Name": "Ally Staging Destination",
    "Section": "Sites",
    "Count": 1,
    "Attributes": {
      "Primary": "Site",
      "Name": "{RetinueAlly.Category:Site Requirement}"
    }
  }
]

How it executes seamlessly:

  • Fixed-Site Allies (Treebeard, Goldberry, Shadowfax): {RetinueAlly.Category:Site Requirement} resolves to an exact site name (e.g. "Wellinghall", "Old Forest", or "Dunharrow; Edoras"). Rule 2 matches the title directly and adds it to the site deck.
  • Regional Allies (Roäc the Raven): {RetinueAlly.Category:Site Requirement} resolves to the dynamic predicate "Region=Northern Rhovanion". Rule 2 evaluates the predicate on the fly and randomly samples a legal site located in Northern Rhovanion (e.g. Dale, The Lonely Mountain, or Buhr Widu).

Avatars (Wizards & Ringwraiths)

Avatars represent the player's physical manifestation on Middle-earth (the five Istari Wizards in Hero decks, or the Ringwraiths in Minion decks). Although indexed with Primary: Character and Secondary: Avatar, they follow unique deck-construction rules: they cannot start in the starting pool and are placed in the Resource play deck. The procedural generator strictly enforces official tournament allowances across all Avatar drafts: up to 3 copies of a single Avatar, or 2 copies of one Avatar and 1 copy of another (including 1 each of three different Avatars, or attempting to draft a 4th Avatar card, is automatically blocked). Because Avatars are foundational keystones around which an entire deck is designed, their taxonomy tagging is intentionally minimal.

1. Tagging Approach & Scope Discipline

Home Region

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Character (Avatar)

Records the canonical territory where the Avatar's fixed home site is located. Assigned strictly to Avatars with a fixed geographic home site.

Representative Examples:
  • Gap of Isen → Derived from Isengard (Saruman)
  • Southern Mirkwood → Derived from Rhosgobel (Radagast)
  • Lindon → Derived from Grey Havens (Círdan / Pallando)
  • Belfalas → Derived from Edhellond (Alatar)
  • [Blank] → Gandalf (prints Home Site: Any Haven, handled dynamically via Home: Site=Haven)
Why Avatar Rules Text is Intentionally OMITTED from Tags:
  • No Role Tags (The Keystone Principle): Wizards feature powerful abilities (such as Saruman taking spells from discard or Radagast drawing cards in Wilderness). Tagging them with Role: Card Flow or Role: Movement is strictly prohibited because generic utility queries in procedural templates (e.g. "Draft 4 Card Flow events") would mistakenly draft 14-Mind Wizard cards into event slots.
  • No Item Affinity Tags: Gandalf's ability to test Gold Rings and Saruman's ability to use Palantíri are not tagged under Target Item. Avatars are drafted intentionally by name or race, never as accessory tools for item queries.
  • No Starting Pool Eligibility: Official game rules prohibit Avatars from starting in the starting pool. The procedural generation engine automatically enforces this constraint regardless of query parameters.

Engine Enforcement of Avatar Copy Limits:
The procedural generation engine actively validates Avatar allowances when evaluating candidate additions in the Resource deck:
  • Hard Cap of 3: Once 3 Avatar cards are added across rules, all subsequent Avatar queries yield 0 additions and report a shortfall.
  • Maximum 2 Distinct Titles: If 2 distinct Avatars are already in the deck (such as 1 Gandalf and 1 Saruman), queries for a third distinct Wizard (e.g. Radagast) are automatically blocked.
  • The 2+1 Balance: When 2 distinct Avatars are drafted, neither is permitted to exceed 2 copies, guaranteeing the resulting Resource deck satisfies the official 2 of one + 1 of another structure.

Characters (Pool & Company Recruitment)

Characters form the core engine of MECCG decks. They are drafted into the Pool (starting company) or Resources (deck characters recruited during play). Because character cards already possess rich official numerical attributes (Mind, Direct, Prowess, Body, Corruption, Skills, and Race), taxonomy tagging on characters is strictly restricted to three structural bridge categories.

1. Tagging Approach & Scope Discipline

Home Region

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Character

Connects the character's printed starting home site to its geographic territory. Enables procedural company drafting rules to recruit companions native to the company leader's home lands.

Representative Examples:
  • Arthedain → Derived from Bree (Aragorn II)
  • Rhudaur → Derived from Rivendell (Elrond)
  • Anórien → Derived from Minas Tirith (Boromir II)
  • Rohan → Derived from Edoras (Théoden)
  • [Blank] → Avatars with dynamic arrival (Gandalf → Home: Site=Haven)

Faction Bonus

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Character

Tracks direct influence bonuses printed on characters for recruiting specific named factions or entire species of factions. Semicolon lists combine named factions with racial predicates.

Representative Examples:
  • Wood-elves → Specific named faction bonus on Thranduil
  • Riders of Rohan → Specific named faction bonus on Théoden
  • Iron Hill Dwarves; Race=Man → Faction and species recruitment bonus on Dáin II
  • All → Universal diplomat wildcard matching any available faction (Adrazar)

Combat Bonus

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Character

Flags characters providing martial advantages—such as direct prowess bonuses, body reduction on enemy strikes, or survival modifications—specifically against targeted creature species. Left blank for general combatants without species-specific text.

Representative Examples:
  • Orc → Prowess and body modifications against Orcs (Dáin II, Gimli)
  • Nazgûl → Combat prowess superiority against Ringwraiths (Éowyn)
What is Intentionally OMITTED on Characters:
  • No Role Tags: Characters are never tagged with Role: Combat, Role: Influence, or Role: Card Flow. Doing so would cause generic event queries to draft characters into resource event slots.
  • No Negative Modifiers: Situational handicaps (such as Barliman Butterbur's -1 influence or Bifur's -1 corruption check) are never tagged. Procedural rules draft characters for their strengths; no rule ever queries a penalty.
  • No Follower Modifiers: Influence over followers is already governed by official Direct capacity and biological Race. Tagging follower perks is redundant.

Factions (Alliances & Marshalling Points)

Factions represent the free peoples, realms, and creature alliances of Middle-earth recruited to aid the struggle against the shadow. They are unique Resource cards worth substantial Marshalling Points (typically 2 to 3 MPs, and up to 6 MPs for legendary storylines). Because factions are recruited during the site phase by diplomats making an influence check, taxonomy tagging focuses on their staging strongholds, storyline prerequisites, and official character locks.

1. Tagging Approach & Scope Discipline

Site Requirement

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Resource (Faction)

Records the mandatory physical stronghold where the faction must be recruited. In The Wizards (METW), all 29 factions recruit at an exact, named site. Guarantees that destination site rules draft legal strongholds automatically.

Representative Examples:
  • Edoras → Staging stronghold for Riders of Rohan
  • Thranduil's Halls → Staging stronghold for Wood-elves
  • Iron Hill Dwarf-hold → Staging stronghold for Iron Hill Dwarves
  • Minas Tirith → Staging stronghold for Tower Guard of Minas Tirith
  • Vale of Erech → Staging destination for Army of the Dead

Prerequisite

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Resource (Faction)

Assigned strictly to storyline factions that require a prerequisite enabler card in play or discard to be mustered. Omitted ([Blank]) for standard factions recruited via normal dice checks.

Representative Example:
  • Paths of the Dead → Mandatory enabler required to muster Army of the Dead

Specific

Classification: Official Database Attribute • Card Scope: Resource (Faction)

Records the exact title of a named unique character required to recruit the faction. Indexed directly in the core catalog (cards-dc.json).

Representative Example:
  • Aragorn II → Named champion required on Army of the Dead
What is Intentionally OMITTED on Factions:
  • No Role Tags: Factions are scored Marshalling Point assets, not utility events. Tagging them with Role: Influence or Role: Recruitment would cause generic event queries to draft high-MP factions into event slots.
  • No Target Species for Standard Modifications: Standard printed dice adjustments (such as Wood-elves granting Elves (+1), Dwarves (-2)) are situational in-game roll modifiers. In deck construction, diplomat recruitment bonuses are handled proactively by Faction Bonus on Character cards.
  • No Redundant Racial Tags: Factions already carry their official species classification in the database (e.g. Wood-elves has Race: Elf, Iron Hill Dwarves has Race: Dwarf, Riders of Rohan has Race: Man). Procedural rules query official Race directly without custom tag duplication.

Hazard Creatures: Standard (Combat & Ambushes)

Standard hazard creatures represent the wandering beasts, Orc warbands, Trolls, Dragons, and guardians that attack companies during the movement/hazard phase. They are identified by Secondary = Creature (excluding dual-mode Nazgûl and split events). Because standard creatures engage companies primarily through physical combat, taxonomy tagging focuses on their keying conditions (terrain, regions, and site holds) and environmental prerequisites.

1. Tagging Approach & Scope Discipline

Region Requirement

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Hazard (Creature)

Identifies canonical terrain types (e.g. Wilderness, Shadow-land) and named regions where the creature can attack companies traveling along their site path, regardless of destination hold type.

Representative Examples:
  • Wilderness → Natural wilderness predators (Cave-drake, Giant, Wargs, Wolves, Brigands, Orc-patrol)
  • Shadow-land; Dark-domain → Shadow realm attackers (Olog-hai (Trolls), Morgul-orc)
  • Heart of Mirkwood; Southern Mirkwood; Woodland Realm → Mirkwood ambushers (Giant Spiders, Huorn)
  • Bay of Belfalas; Mouths of the Anduin; Andrast Coast → Coastal raiders (Corsairs of Umbar)
  • Withered Heath; Grey Mountain Narrows; Northern Rhovanion → Wandering Unique Dragons (Agburanar, Daelomin, Smaug)
  • [Blank] → Pûkel-men (keyed strictly to Ruins & Lairs in southern regions; omitted to prevent dead draws in non-ruins destinations)

Site Requirement

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Hazard (Creature)

Indexes universal printed site hold types (Dark-hold, Shadow-hold, Ruins & Lairs) or explicitly printed named sites where the creature can attack during the site phase.

Representative Examples:
  • Dark-hold; Shadow-hold → Universal stronghold defenders (Olog-hai (Trolls), Barrow-wight, Ghosts, Ghouls, Orc-guard, Orc-watch, Silent Watcher, Pûkel-men)
  • Ruins & Lairs → Universal ruins lurkers (Cave-drake, Giant)
  • The Lonely Mountain → Literal printed home lair for Smaug
  • Dancing Spire → Literal printed home lair for Daelomin
  • Drúadan Forest; Old Forest; Wellinghall → Printed wood havens for Awakened Plant creatures (Huorn, Old Man Willow)

Environment Stance

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Hazard (Creature)

Assigned strictly to creatures whose roaming attacks require Doors of Night to be in play.

Representative Example:
  • Doors of Night → Mandatory roaming enabler for the four Unique Dragons (Agburanar, Daelomin, Leucaruth, Smaug)

Role

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Hazard (Creature)

Standard creatures do not carry Role: Combat because their combat role is already indexed by official stats (Prowess, Strikes, Body). Assigned strictly to non-damaging utility creatures specializing in item theft.

Representative Examples:
  • Item → Specialized item pickpockets (Pick-pocket, Thief)
  • [Blank] → All standard combat creatures (Orcs, Trolls, Spiders, Wolves, Dragons, Undead)
Pragmatic Deck-Building Trade-off — Region-Bounded Hold Clauses:
Several creatures print secondary clauses allowing them to be keyed to specific hold types within listed regions (for example, Corsairs of Umbar at Ruins & Lairs and Shadow-holds in coastal regions; Giant Spiders at Ruins, Shadow, and Dark-holds in Mirkwood; and Pûkel-men at Ruins & Lairs in 10 southern regions).

Why we omit these conditional holds from Site Requirement:
  • Preventing Geographic Leakage: If Corsairs, Giant Spiders, or Pûkel-men were tagged with generic Site Requirement: Ruins & Lairs, a procedural rule drafting Ruins guardians for an Eriador or Northern Rhovanion deck would draft them to guard Weathertop or Dale, where they are illegal.
  • Avoiding Expansion Maintenance Debt: Manually resolving those clauses into specific site titles (e.g. Amon Hen, Glittering Caves) only works for a single set and requires manual updates every time a later expansion introduces a new Ruins & Lairs in those regions.
  • How They Draft Cleanly: Roaming regional creatures (Corsairs, Giant Spiders, Huorn, Old Man Willow) are drafted into local theater decks via Region Requirement, while Pûkel-men is drafted cleanly via its unconditional base holds (Dark-hold; Shadow-hold).

Hazard Creatures: Dual-Mode (Nazgûl & Split Events)

Dual-mode hazard cards possess split timing: they can enter play either as a regional hazard creature or as an ongoing permanent-event (or reactive short-event). In The Wizards, this partition consists of the nine Ringwraith Nazgûl, Shelob, and the Mouth of Sauron. Because these cards perform double duty, taxonomy tagging balances their strict regional creature boundaries with their global utility event mechanisms.

1. Tagging Approach & Scope Discipline

Region Requirement

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Hazard (Dual-Mode Creature)

Tags strictly the printed territories and terrain symbols where the card is legally allowed to attack as a creature. Never tag All for their globally playable event mode; doing so would cause the deck generator to draft them as creatures in illegal regions like the Shire.

Representative Examples:
  • Harondor; Horse Plains; Gorgoroth; Khand → Permitted creature territories for Akhôrahil
  • Imlad Morgul; Gorgoroth → Permitted creature territories for Shelob
  • Dark-domain; Gorgoroth; Udûn → Permitted creature territories for The Witch-king of Angmar

Site Requirement

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Hazard (Dual-Mode Creature)

All nine Nazgûl and the Mouth of Sauron print [d] as their universal base site hold key.

Representative Examples:
  • Dark-hold → Base hold key for all 9 Nazgûl and Mouth of Sauron
  • [Blank] → Shelob (attacks bounded strictly by her listed regions, Imlad Morgul and Gorgoroth)

Environment Stance

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Hazard (Dual-Mode Creature)

Assigned strictly when an ongoing event mode requires Doors of Night to be in play.

Representative Example:
  • Doors of Night → Required to activate the permanent-event mode of Shelob

Role

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Hazard (Dual-Mode Creature)

Classifies what the card's event mode accomplishes when resolved or tapped.

Representative Examples:
  • Readiness → Tapping characters (Adûnaphel)
  • Combat → Direct body debuffs (Akhôrahil), extra strikes (Hoarmûrath of Dír), or minion swarm attack buffs (Dwar of Waw, Shelob)
  • Item → Forcing item discards on wounded characters (Indûr Dawndeath)
  • Card Flow → Hand disruption (Khamûl the Easterling) or hazard retrieval from discard (Mouth of Sauron, Ûvatha the Horseman)
  • Corruption → Universal corruption checks on tapped characters (Ren the Unclean)
  • Site → Hold transformation (The Witch-king of Angmar)

Target Species

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Hazard (Dual-Mode Creature)

Assigned strictly when the card's event mode specifically buffs or aids targeted minion species.

Representative Examples:
  • Animal; Wolf → Animal and wolf swarm bonuses on Dwar of Waw
  • Spider → Spider combat buffs on Shelob
  • [Blank] → Nazgûl targeting individual company members directly

Hazard Events (Disruption, Environment & Traps)

Hazard events represent the pervasive influence of the Shadow across Middle-earth: severe weather, corrupting whispers, ambushes at ancient strongholds, and traps laid for specific peoples or treasures. Identified by Primary = Hazard and Secondary = Short-event; Long-event; Permanent-event (excluding dual-mode Nazgûl and split creatures), this partition contains 60 distinct cards in The Wizards (16 Permanent-events, 15 Long-events, and 29 Short-events). Because hazard events do not have physical combat stats, taxonomy tagging defines their tactical role, environmental conditions, pathway constraints, and targeted items or species.

1. Tagging Approach & Scope Discipline

Role

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Hazard (Event)

The primary classifier governing what tactical disruption the hazard event executes. Semicolon lists define modal choices or dual effects.

Representative Examples:
  • Automatic Attack → Modifies, doubles prowess/strikes, or adds automatic attacks at sites (Awaken Denizens, Awaken Minions, Eye of Sauron, Arouse Denizens, Arouse Minions, Siege, Balrog of Moria)
  • Combat → Buffs creature prowess/strikes, adds strikes, or inflates hazard limits (Clouds, Minions Stir, Plague of Wights, Wake of War, Fell Beast, Fury of the Iron Crown, Gloom, Lost in...)
  • Corruption → Inflicts corruption points (CP) or forces corruption checks (Despair of the Heart, Lure of..., Greed, The Precious, The Ring's Betrayal, The Will of the Ring, The Balance of Things, Weariness of the Heart)
  • Duration → Extends the life of ongoing long-events (The Will of Sauron)
  • Influence → Discards recruited factions by testing unused general influence (Muster Disperses)
  • Item → Forces item discards on moving or visited companies (Drowning Seas)
  • Movement → Forces companies to return to origin or halts transit (Foul Fumes, Long Winter, Snowstorm, Drowning Seas, Siege)
  • Readiness → Taps characters or bounces them to hand (Call of Home, Call of the Sea, Tookish Blood, New Moon, Morgul-horse)
  • Site → Taps sites, closes sites, or cancels the site phase (River, Lost at Sea, Lost in Free-domains, Storms of Ossë, Balrog of Moria)
  • Terrain → Converts region path symbols and terrain types between safe and hostile (Fell Winter, Morgul Night, Awaken the Earth's Fire, Choking Shadows, Gloom, New Moon)
  • Card Flow → Cancels searching (Bane of the Ithil-stone), retrieves hazard events (The Nazgûl are Abroad), forces discards (Drowning Seas), or cancels environmental cards (Twilight)

Environment Stance

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Hazard (Event)

Assigned to cards that require Doors of Night to be in play, or gain enhanced lethality when active (17 cards across METW), as well as Doors of Night itself.

Representative Examples:
  • Doors of Night → Foundational shadow anchor (Doors of Night) and dependent dark weather/events (Foul Fumes, Morgul Night, Long Winter, Snowstorm, Gloom, Choking Shadows)
  • [Blank] → Standard events playable in clear weather

Region Requirement

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Hazard (Event)

Assigned when an event triggers on, requires, or targets specific terrain in the company's site path, or buffs attacks across specific named territories.

Representative Examples:
  • Wilderness; Border-land → Terrain required for travel hazards (Snowstorm, Long Winter)
  • Coastal Sea → Ocean terrain hazards (Lost at Sea, Drowning Seas)
  • Hollin; Redhorn Gate; Wold & Foothills → Regional territories buffed by Balrog of Moria (Permanent-event)

Site Requirement

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Hazard (Event)

Assigned when an event attaches to, modifies, or requires a company to be located at a specific hold type or site. Events with open global modes alongside optional hold keys (e.g. Dragon's Desolation) leave this field blank.

Representative Examples:
  • Ruins & Lairs → Target hold for Awaken Denizens / Arouse Denizens
  • Dark-hold; Shadow-hold → Target hold for Awaken Minions / Arouse Minions
  • Border-hold; Free-hold → Target hold for Siege
  • Lórien; Moria → Named sites affected by Balrog of Moria

Target Species

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Hazard (Event)

Identifies biological minion races escalated by combat events, targeted character races, or printed species immunity predicates.

Representative Examples:
  • Orc; Troll → Minion swarm attack buffs (Minions Stir, Wake of War, Fury of the Iron Crown)
  • Undead → Attack escalation for spirits and wights (Plague of Wights)
  • Elf → Targeted character species penalty (The Burden of Time, Call of the Sea)
  • Hobbit → Targeted character corruption (Tookish Blood)
  • Race!=Hobbit && Race!=Wizard → Printed character exclusions on corruption hazards (Despair of the Heart, Lure of Nature, Lure of Power, Greed)

Target Item

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Hazard (Event)

Assigned strictly when an event specifically targets, punishes, or triggers off an item classification. Enables procedural rules to draft anti-Ring or anti-Palantír hazard packages.

Representative Examples:
  • Ring → Anti-Ring disruption (The Nazgûl are Abroad, The Precious, The Ring's Betrayal, The Will of the Ring)
  • Palantír → Seeing stone corruption (Bane of the Ithil-stone)

Items & Gear (Weapons, Relics & Hoards)

Items represent martial arms, protective armor, quest relics, seeing stones, and magical rings carried by characters to enhance their prowess, direct influence, and resilience, or to score Marshalling Points (MPs). Partitioned across five official categories (Minor Item, Major Item, Greater Item, Gold Ring Item, and Special Item), items enter play during the site phase at locations with matching item-playability symbols. Because general item playability is already governed by official discrete site attributes (MinorItem, MajorItem, GreaterItem, GoldRing, Hoard), custom taxonomy tagging focuses strictly on functional gameplay roles, quest prerequisites, species affinities, and mandatory play/storage locations.

1. Tagging Approach & Scope Discipline

Role

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Resource (Item)

Classifies the primary tactical game mechanism executed by the item when borne, tapped, or expended.

Representative Examples:
  • Combat → Weapons granting prowess, armor/shields granting body, or combat damage debuffs (Glamdring, Orcrist, Durin's Axe, Hauberk of Bright Mail, Black Arrow, Miruvor)
  • Combat; Protection → Gear providing both physical combat stats and attack/strike cancellation (Great-shield of Rohan, Star-glass)
  • Protection → Pure defensive strike evasion, attack cancellation, or environmental stealth (Elven Cloak, Torque of Hues, Magic Ring of Nature, Magic Ring of Stealth)
  • Readiness → Wound healing and untapping stamina (Athelas, Healing Herbs)
  • Card Flow → Hand size expansion, deck inspection, deck reordering, or card tutoring (Palantír of Amon Sûl, Palantír of Annúminas, Palantír of Minas Tirith, Palantír of Orthanc, Book of Mazarbul)
  • Card Flow; Protection → Continuous hazard permanent-event cancellation (Palantír of Osgiliath)
  • Corruption → Active corruption removal (Palantír of Elostirion)
  • Influence → Mathematical bonuses to Direct Influence (DI) or recruitment check rolls (Elf-stone, Horn of Anor, Lesser Ring, Magic Ring of Words)
  • Influence; Readiness → Dwarf influence leadership and stamina refreshing (The Arkenstone)
  • Influence; Recruitment → Direct faction recruitment tools and homeland character influence (Red Arrow)
  • Item → Modifying Gold Ring test rolls or searching play decks for items (Scroll of Isildur, Dwarven Rings of Barin, Bávor, Drúin, Dwálin, Thélor, Thrár)
  • Site → Untapping visited site cards (Dwarven Ring of Durin's Tribe)
  • Quest → Relics and storyline items delivering victory points when stored or planted at designated strongholds (Earth of Galadriel's Orchard, Sapling of the White Tree, Book of Mazarbul)
  • [Blank] → Untested Gold Rings (Beautiful Gold Ring, Fair Gold Ring, Precious Gold Ring; left blank so generic role queries do not draft untested rings into event slots)

Site Requirement

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Resource (Item)

Standard items are playable at any site matching their official tier (Minor, Major, Greater, Hoard) and leave Site Requirement [Blank]. This category is strictly reserved for unique items with an explicitly printed mandatory play or scoring location. Semicolon lists record connected storyline journey paths (e.g. play site + storage stronghold).

Representative Examples:
  • Lórien; Bag End → Earth of Galadriel's Orchard (played at Lórien, delivered/stored at Bag End for 2 MPs)
  • Moria → Mandatory entry site for Book of Mazarbul
  • Wellinghall → Mandatory entry site for Ent-draughts
  • Bag End → Mandatory entry site for Red Book of Westmarch
  • Isengard → Mandatory entry site for Palantír of Orthanc
  • Minas Tirith → Mandatory entry site for Palantír of Minas Tirith
  • The White Towers → Mandatory entry site for Palantír of Elostirion
  • [Blank] → Standard Minor, Major, Greater, Ring, and Hoard items (drafted via discrete site flags like MajorItem: yes)

Region Requirement

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Resource (Item)

Assigned when an item's defensive capability or activation trigger is strictly bound to a canonical terrain type or geographic territory.

Representative Example:
  • Wilderness → Terrain strike-cancellation boundary on Elven Cloak

Prerequisite For

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Resource (Item)

Identifies the exact quest or storyline card that requires this relic. Allows generic equipment queries to shield themselves using "Prerequisite For": "[Blank]" so niche storyline relics are not drafted into decks lacking their parent quest.

Representative Examples:
  • Andúril, the Flame of the West → Enabled by Narsil
  • The White Tree → Enabled by Sapling of the White Tree
  • Cracks of Doom → Destroyed by the quest on The One Ring

Prerequisite

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Resource (Item)

Assigned strictly when an item legally mandates another named card to enter play.

Representative Example:
  • Ents of Fangorn; Leaflock; Quickbeam; Skinbark; Treebeard → Required active ally/faction on Ent-draughts

Target Species

Classification: Custom Category • Card Scope: Resource (Item)

Indexes biological species that the item explicitly targets in combat, grants dedicated class/prowess perks to, or legally requires to be borne/used.

Representative Examples:
  • Dúnadan → Mandatory bearer race for Athelas
  • Man → Bearer race required to retain Black Arrow
  • Dwarf → Species bonus and check enabler on Durin's Axe, The Arkenstone, and all seven Dwarven Rings
  • Hobbit → Bearer prowess boost on Sting and recruitment check bonus on Red Book of Westmarch
  • Orc → Martial combat bonus on Glamdring and Orcrist
  • Animal; Spider; Undead; Wolf → Monster attack cancellation and prowess reduction on Star-glass
  • Man; Orc; Troll → Attack cancellation on The Iron Crown
  • Elf; Wizard → Targeted corruption healing on Palantír of Elostirion
  • Wizard → Mandatory bearer race for Wizard's Ring
What is Intentionally OMITTED on Items:
  • No Redundant Site Requirement on Standard Items: Standard Minor, Major, Greater, Ring, and Hoard items leave Site Requirement [Blank]. They are drafted to legal adventure sites dynamically using Cardnum's discrete item attributes (e.g. "MajorItem": "yes" or "Hoard": "yes"). Manually listing hold types on items would break automated site discovery.
  • No Soft Perks as Hard Dependencies (Red Arrow): Although Red Arrow provides direct influence bonuses to recruit Riders of Rohan, the Riders do not require the arrow to be mustered. Tagging Red Arrow with Prerequisite For: Riders of Rohan would falsely lock a valuable 2-MP Major Item out of generic company decks.
  • No Situational Perks as Primary Roles (Athelas): While Aragorn can remove corruption with Athelas, its baseline mechanic is wound recovery by any Dúnadan. Tagging it with Role: Corruption would cause generic corruption-defense rules to draft it into non-Aragorn companies where it cannot remove corruption.
