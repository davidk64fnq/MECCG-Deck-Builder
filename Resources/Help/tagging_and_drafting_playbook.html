Allies (Resources & Company Retinue)

Allies are unique Resource entities that accompany a company like characters, but do not count toward company size limits, cannot bear items, and are controlled without Direct Influence. They provide valuable Marshalling Points (MPs), tactical movement acceleration, strike evasion, or alternative faction recruitment. Because allies enter play at specific geographic locations during the site phase, taxonomy tagging focuses on their staging destinations and tactical mechanisms.

1. Tagging Approach & Scope Discipline

Category Permitted Values Tagging Logic & Omission Rules
Site Requirement Exact catalog site titles (e.g. Old Forest, Wellinghall, Bag End; Bree, Dunharrow; Edoras) or dynamic site predicates (Region=Northern Rhovanion).
  • Mandatory staging locations required to bring the ally into play.
  • Semicolon multi-values list alternative legal staging sites.
  • Dynamic Predicates for Regional Allies: Allies playable at any site in a region (such as Roäc the Raven) carry Region=Northern Rhovanion. This enables universal site rules (e.g. "Name": "{Ally.Category:Site Requirement}") to query the site deck for a legal destination with zero fallbacks.
Region Requirement Geographic territory required to play the ally (e.g. Northern Rhovanion).
  • Used when an ally's recruitment is geographically tied to a specific territory (Roäc the Raven), enabling Geographic Theater rules to discover regional allies.
  • Post-Entry Roaming Limits: Geographic discard boundaries (such as Ents or Tom Bombadil discarding when moving outside their home lands) are omitted here to prevent false drafting into decks that lack their actual staging sites.
Role Movement, Protection, Recruitment.
  • Movement: Extra movement phases, emergency Haven relocation, or unrestricted flight (Bill the Pony, Gwaihir, Shadowfax).
  • Protection: Strike immunity against automatic attacks, attack evasion, or hazard cancellation (Treebeard, Goldberry, Tom Bombadil).
  • Recruitment: Alternative faction recruitment mechanisms (Roäc the Raven).
What is Intentionally OMITTED on Allies:
  • No Home Attribute: Allies are resource cards, not characters. They possess no printed starting site or healing haven. Staging sites belong strictly under Site Requirement.
  • No Role: Combat on Defensive Allies: Ents and nature spirits possess high defensive prowess and strike immunity, but do not grant combat prowess bonuses or extra strikes to other characters. Classifying them as Role: Combat would pollute combat trick filters.
  • No Hard Dependencies for Soft Triggers (*Gollum*): Although Gollum can discard The One Ring, he is an autonomous defensive ally. Under the Primary Usability Rule, he is never tagged with Specific: The One Ring, ensuring open utility rules can draft him safely.

Avatars (Wizards & Ringwraiths)

Avatars represent the player's physical manifestation on Middle-earth (the five Istari Wizards in Hero decks, or the Ringwraiths in Minion decks). Although indexed with Primary: Character and Secondary: Avatar, they follow unique deck-construction rules: they cannot start in the starting pool and are placed in the Resource play deck. The procedural generator strictly enforces official tournament allowances across all Avatar drafts: up to 3 copies of a single Avatar, or 2 copies of one Avatar and 1 copy of another (including 1 each of three different Avatars, or attempting to draft a 4th Avatar card, is automatically blocked). Because Avatars are foundational keystones around which an entire deck is designed, their taxonomy tagging is intentionally minimal.

1. Tagging Approach & Scope Discipline

Category Permitted Values Tagging Logic & Omission Rules
Home Region Canonical territory where the Avatar's fixed home site is located (e.g. Gap of Isen for Isengard, Southern Mirkwood for Rhosgobel, Lindon for Grey Havens, Belfalas for Edhellond).
  • Assigned strictly to Avatars with a fixed geographic home site.
  • The Gandalf Exception ([Blank]): Gandalf prints Home Site: Any Haven, indexed in the official database as the native predicate Home: Site=Haven. Because Gandalf has open starting and arrival options across Middle-earth, his Home Region is left [Blank] to prevent tying him to a single territory.
Why Avatar Rules Text is Intentionally OMITTED from Tags:
  • No Role Tags (The Keystone Principle): Wizards feature powerful abilities (such as Saruman taking spells from discard or Radagast drawing cards in Wilderness). Tagging them with Role: Card Flow or Role: Movement is strictly prohibited because generic utility queries in procedural templates (e.g. "Draft 4 Card Flow events") would mistakenly draft 14-Mind Wizard cards into event slots.
  • No Item Affinity Tags: Gandalf's ability to test Gold Rings and Saruman's ability to use Palantíri are not tagged under Target Item. Avatars are drafted intentionally by name or race, never as accessory tools for item queries.
  • No Starting Pool Eligibility: Official game rules prohibit Avatars from starting in the starting pool. The procedural generation engine automatically enforces this constraint regardless of query parameters.

Engine Enforcement of Avatar Copy Limits:
The procedural generation engine actively validates Avatar allowances when evaluating candidate additions in the Resource deck:

  • Maximum 2 Distinct Titles: If 2 distinct Avatars are already in the deck (such as 1 Gandalf and 1 Saruman), queries for a third distinct Wizard (e.g. Radagast) are automatically blocked.
  • The 2+1 Balance: When 2 distinct Avatars are drafted, neither is permitted to exceed 2 copies, guaranteeing the resulting Resource deck satisfies the official 2 of one + 1 of another structure.

Characters (Pool & Company Recruitment)

Characters form the core engine of MECCG decks. They are drafted into the Pool (starting company) or Resources (deck characters recruited during play). Because character cards already possess rich official numerical attributes (Mind, Direct, Prowess, Body, Corruption, Skills, and Race), taxonomy tagging on characters is strictly restricted to three structural bridge categories.

1. Tagging Approach & Scope Discipline

Category Permitted Values Tagging Logic & Omission Rules
Home Region Canonical region name where the character's printed home site is located (e.g. Arthedain for Bree, Rhudaur for Rivendell).
  • Enables regional company drafting matching the leader's home territory.
  • Avatars & Dynamic Homes: Wizards and Ringwraiths with open starting locations (e.g. Any Haven) leave Home Region [Blank]. Their open arrival is handled by the native predicate Home: Site=Haven.
Faction Bonus Exact catalog faction titles (e.g. Riders of Rohan, Wood-elves) or species predicates (Race=Dwarf, Race=Man, All).
  • Captures direct influence bonuses printed on characters.
  • Semicolon multi-values combine specific factions and racial bonuses (e.g. Iron Hill Dwarves; Race=Man on Dáin II).
  • Universal diplomats use the All wildcard (Adrazar).
Combat Bonus Target creature species against which the character has combat superiority (e.g. Orc, Nazgûl).
  • Captures character prowess boosts or enemy body reduction (e.g. Éowyn vs. Nazgûl).
  • Leave blank for general combatants without species-specific text.
What is Intentionally OMITTED on Characters:
  • No Role Tags: Characters are never tagged with Role: Combat, Role: Influence, or Role: Card Flow. Doing so would cause generic event queries to draft characters into resource event slots.
  • No Negative Modifiers: Situational handicaps (such as Barliman Butterbur's -1 influence or Bifur's -1 corruption check) are never tagged. Procedural rules draft characters for their strengths; no rule ever queries a penalty.
  • No Follower Modifiers: Influence over followers is already governed by official Direct capacity and biological Race. Tagging follower perks is redundant.

Factions (Alliances & Marshalling Points)

Factions represent the free peoples, realms, and creature alliances of Middle-earth recruited to aid the struggle against the shadow. They are unique Resource cards worth substantial Marshalling Points (typically 2 to 3 MPs, and up to 6 MPs for legendary storylines). Because factions are recruited during the site phase by diplomats making an influence check, taxonomy tagging focuses on their staging strongholds, storyline prerequisites, and official character locks.

1. Tagging Approach & Scope Discipline

Category / Attribute Permitted Values Tagging Logic & Scope Rules
Site Requirement
Custom Category		 Exact catalog site title where the faction must be recruited (e.g. Edoras, Thranduil's Halls, Iron Hill Dwarf-hold, Minas Tirith, Vale of Erech).
  • Mandatory physical stronghold required to make the recruitment influence check.
  • In The Wizards (METW), all 29 factions recruit at an exact, named site.
  • Guarantees that destination site rules ("Name": "{Faction.Category:Site Requirement}") draft legal strongholds automatically.
Prerequisite
Custom Category		 Named enabler card required to play or muster the faction (e.g. Paths of the Dead for Army of the Dead).
  • Assigned strictly to storyline factions that require a prerequisite card in play or discard to be mustered.
  • Omitted ([Blank]) for standard factions recruited via normal dice checks.
Specific
Official Attribute		 Exact title of a named unique character required to recruit this faction (e.g. Aragorn II on Army of the Dead).
  • Indexed directly in the core catalog (cards-dc.json).
  • Enables procedural generation rules to query "Name": "{Faction.Specific}" to recruit the required hero into the starting pool without hardcoding.
  • Left unpopulated on standard open factions.
What is Intentionally OMITTED on Factions:
  • No Role Tags: Factions are scored Marshalling Point assets, not utility events. Tagging them with Role: Influence or Role: Recruitment would cause generic event queries to draft high-MP factions into event slots.
  • No Target Species for Standard Modifications: Standard printed dice adjustments (such as Wood-elves granting Elves (+1), Dwarves (-2)) are situational in-game roll modifiers. In deck construction, diplomat recruitment bonuses are handled proactively by Faction Bonus on Character cards.
  • No Redundant Racial Tags: Factions already carry their official species classification in the database (e.g. Wood-elves has Race: Elf, Iron Hill Dwarves has Race: Dwarf, Riders of Rohan has Race: Man). Procedural rules query official Race directly without custom tag duplication.

Hazard Creatures: Standard (Combat & Ambushes)

Standard hazard creatures represent the wandering beasts, Orc warbands, Trolls, Dragons, and guardians that attack companies during the movement/hazard phase. They are identified by Secondary = Creature (excluding dual-mode Nazgûl and split events). Because standard creatures engage companies primarily through physical combat, taxonomy tagging focuses on their keying conditions (terrain, regions, and site holds) and environmental prerequisites.

1. Tagging Approach & Scope Discipline

Category / Attribute Permitted Values Tagging Logic & Scope Rules
Region Requirement
Custom Category		 Canonical terrain types (Wilderness, Border-land, Shadow-land, Dark-domain, Free-domain, Coastal Sea) and named regions where the creature can be keyed to the region itself (e.g. Heart of Mirkwood, Grey Mountain Narrows, Andrast Coast).
  • Assigned when a creature can be keyed directly to a terrain symbol or named region in the company's site path, regardless of the destination site's hold type.
  • Pragmatic Omission (*Pûkel-men*): While *Corsairs* and *Giant Spiders* can be keyed directly to their listed regions (attacking even if the destination is a Free-hold or Border-hold), *Pûkel-men* can only be keyed to Ruins & Lairs destination sites within its 10 southern regions. To prevent *Pûkel-men* from being drafted as a dead card into Gondor or Rohan decks that only visit Free-holds and Border-holds (*Minas Tirith*, *Edoras*), its Region Requirement is left [Blank].
Site Requirement
Custom Category		 Universal printed site hold types (Dark-hold, Shadow-hold, Ruins & Lairs, Border-hold, Free-hold) or explicitly printed named sites (e.g. Caves of Ûlund, Dancing Spire, Irerock, The Lonely Mountain, Moria, Drúadan Forest; Old Forest; Wellinghall).
  • Universal Holds: Tag hold types only if the creature can attack at that hold type unconditionally anywhere on Middle-earth.
  • Literal Named Sites: Tag specific site names only if the site title is explicitly printed on the face of the card. (Never manually expand regional hold permissions into hardcoded site lists).
  • Pragmatic Omission of Region-Bounded Holds: Secondary clauses such as "also playable at Ruins & Lairs in these regions" (on *Corsairs*, *Giant Spiders*, *Huorn*, *Old Man Willow*, and *Pûkel-men*) are omitted from Site Requirement so generic hold queries do not draft them into illegal regions.
Environment Stance
Custom Category		 Doors of Night
  • Assigned strictly to the four Unique Dragons (*Agburanar*, *Daelomin*, *Leucaruth*, *Smaug*) that require Doors of Night to roam outside their fixed lairs.
  • Left [Blank] on all standard non-environmental creatures.
Role
Custom Category		 Item
  • Omitted on Combatants: Standard creatures do not carry Role: Combat. Their combat nature is already fully represented by official stats (Prowess, Strikes, Body). Tagging them with Combat would pollute event filters when drafting combat tricks.
  • Pure Utility Exceptions: Assigned strictly to non-damaging utility creatures that specialize purely in item theft: *Pick-pocket* and *Thief*.
Pragmatic Deck-Building Trade-off — Region-Bounded Hold Clauses:
Several creatures print secondary clauses allowing them to be keyed to specific hold types within listed regions (for example, *Corsairs of Umbar* at Ruins & Lairs and Shadow-holds in coastal regions; *Giant Spiders* at Ruins, Shadow, and Dark-holds in Mirkwood; and *Pûkel-men* at Ruins & Lairs in 10 southern regions).

Why we omit these conditional holds from Site Requirement:
  • Preventing Geographic Leakage: If *Corsairs*, *Giant Spiders*, or *Pûkel-men* were tagged with generic Site Requirement: Ruins & Lairs, a procedural rule drafting Ruins guardians for an Eriador or Northern Rhovanion deck would draft them to guard *Weathertop* or *Dale*, where they are illegal.
  • Avoiding Expansion Maintenance Debt: Manually resolving those clauses into specific site titles (e.g. *Amon Hen*, *Glittering Caves*) only works for a single set and requires manual updates every time a later expansion introduces a new Ruins & Lairs in those regions.
  • How They Draft Cleanly: Roaming regional creatures (*Corsairs*, *Giant Spiders*, *Huorn*, *Old Man Willow*) are drafted into local theater decks via Region Requirement, while *Pûkel-men* is drafted cleanly via its unconditional base holds (Dark-hold; Shadow-hold).

Hazard Creatures: Dual-Mode (Nazgûl & Split Events)

Dual-mode hazard cards possess split timing: they can enter play either as a regional hazard creature or as an ongoing permanent-event (or reactive short-event). In The Wizards, this partition consists of the nine Ringwraith Nazgûl, *Shelob*, and the *Mouth of Sauron*. Because these cards perform double duty, taxonomy tagging balances their strict regional creature boundaries with their global utility event mechanisms.

1. Tagging Approach & Scope Discipline

Category / Attribute Permitted Values Tagging Logic & Scope Rules
Region Requirement
Custom Category		 Exact geographic territories and terrain symbols printed on the card face for creature entry (e.g. Gorgoroth, Dark-domain, Harondor, Imlad Morgul).
  • Creature-Side Only: Tag strictly the printed regions where the card is legally allowed to attack as a creature.
  • Never Tag All for Events: Although their event mode can be played globally, tagging "All" would cause the deck generator to falsely draft them as creatures in illegal regions (such as the Shire or Rhudaur).
Site Requirement
Custom Category		 Dark-hold
  • All nine Nazgûl and *Mouth of Sauron* print [d] as their universal base hold key.
  • Left [Blank] on *Shelob*, whose site attacks are bounded strictly by her listed regions (Imlad Morgul and Gorgoroth).
Environment Stance
Custom Category		 Doors of Night
  • Assigned strictly to *Shelob*, whose event mode requires Doors of Night to be in play.
  • Left [Blank] on the Nazgûl and *Mouth of Sauron*.
Role
Custom Category		 Combat, Card Flow, Corruption, Item, Readiness, Site
  • Classifies the Event Mode: Unlike pure creatures, dual cards carry a tactical role tag defining what their event side accomplishes when tapped or resolved.
  • Readiness → Tapping characters (*Adûnaphel*).
  • Combat → Direct body debuffs (*Akhôrahil*), extra strikes (*Hoarmûrath of Dír*), or creature attack buffs (*Dwar of Waw*, *Shelob*).
  • Item → Forcing item discards on wounded characters (*Indûr Dawndeath*).
  • Card Flow → Hand disruption (*Khamûl the Easterling*) or hazard retrieval from discard (*Mouth of Sauron*, *Ûvatha the Horseman*).
  • Corruption → Universal corruption checks (*Ren the Unclean*).
  • Site → Hold transformation (*Witch-king of Angmar*).
Target Species
Custom Category		 Animal; Spider; Wolf
  • Assigned strictly when the card's event mode specifically buffs or aids targeted minion species (*Dwar of Waw* and *Shelob*).
  • Left [Blank] on all other dual cards.

Hazard Events (Disruption, Environment & Traps)

Hazard events represent the pervasive influence of the Shadow across Middle-earth: severe weather, corrupting whispers, ambushes at ancient strongholds, and traps laid for specific peoples or treasures. Identified by Primary = Hazard and Secondary = Short-event; Long-event; Permanent-event (excluding dual-mode Nazgûl and split creatures), this partition contains 60 distinct cards in The Wizards (16 Permanent-events, 15 Long-events, and 29 Short-events). Because hazard events do not have physical combat stats, taxonomy tagging defines their tactical role, environmental conditions, pathway constraints, and targeted items or species.

1. Tagging Approach & Scope Discipline

Category / Attribute Permitted Values Tagging Logic & Scope Rules
Role
Custom Category		 Automatic Attack, Card Flow, Combat, Corruption, Duration, Influence, Item, Movement, Readiness, Site, Terrain
  • The primary classifier governing what tactical disruption the hazard event executes. Semicolon multi-values are assigned when an event provides modal choices or dual effects.
  • Automatic Attack: Modifies, doubles prowess/strikes, or adds automatic attacks at sites (*Awaken Denizens*, *Awaken Minions*, *Eye of Sauron*, *Arouse Denizens*, *Arouse Minions*, *Siege*, *Balrog of Moria*).
  • Combat: Buffs creature prowess/strikes, adds strikes, or inflates the hazard limit (*Clouds*, *Minions Stir*, *Plague of Wights*, *Wake of War*, *Fell Beast*, *Fury of the Iron Crown*, *Gloom*, *Lost in...*).
  • Corruption: Inflicts corruption points (CP) or forces corruption checks (*Despair of the Heart*, *Lure of...*, *Greed*, *The Precious*, *The Ring's Betrayal*, *The Will of the Ring*, *The Balance of Things*, *Weariness of the Heart*).
  • Duration: Extends the life of ongoing long-events (*The Will of Sauron*).
  • Influence: Discards recruited factions by testing the opponent's unused general influence (*Muster Disperses*).
  • Item: Forces item discards on moving or visited companies (*Drowning Seas*).
  • Movement: Forces companies to return to their site of origin or halts movement (*Foul Fumes*, *Long Winter*, *Snowstorm*, *Drowning Seas*, *Siege*).
  • Readiness: Taps characters or bounces them back to hand (*Call of Home*, *Call of the Sea*, *Tookish Blood*, *New Moon*, *Morgul-horse*).
  • Site: Taps sites, closes sites, or cancels the site phase (*River*, *Lost at Sea*, *Lost in Free-domains*, *Storms of Ossë*, *Balrog of Moria*).
  • Terrain: Converts region path symbols and territory terrain classifications between safe and hostile (*Fell Winter*, *Morgul Night*, *Awaken the Earth's Fire*, *Choking Shadows*, *Gloom*, *New Moon*).
  • Card Flow: Cancels deck/discard searching (*Bane of the Ithil-stone*), returns events from discard (*The Nazgûl are Abroad*), forces hand discards (*Drowning Seas*), or cancels environment events in play (*Twilight*).
Environment Stance
Custom Category		 Doors of Night
  • Assigned to cards that require Doors of Night to be in play, or gain enhanced lethality when Doors of Night is active (17 cards across METW).
  • Also assigned to Doors of Night itself as the foundational Shadow anchor.
  • Enables procedural deck rules to automatically recruit Doors of Night whenever weather or environmental hazards are drafted.
Region Requirement
Custom Category		 Canonical terrain types (Wilderness, Border-land, Shadow-land, Dark-domain, Coastal Sea, Free-domain) or specific regional territories (e.g. Hollin; Redhorn Gate; Wold & Foothills).
  • Assigned when an event triggers on, requires, or targets specific terrain in the company's site path (e.g. *Snowstorm*, *Long Winter*, *Foul Fumes*, *Lost at Sea*, and the *Lost in...* series).
  • Assigned on regional permanent-events like *Balrog of Moria* that buff all automatic attacks across specific named territories.
Site Requirement
Custom Category		 Structural hold types (Ruins & Lairs, Dark-hold; Shadow-hold, Border-hold; Free-hold) or specific named sites (Lórien; Moria).
  • Assigned when an event attaches to, modifies, or requires a company to be at a specific hold type or site.
  • *Awaken Denizens* / *Arouse Denizens* → Ruins & Lairs.
  • *Awaken Minions* / *Arouse Minions* → Dark-hold; Shadow-hold.
  • *Siege* → Border-hold; Free-hold.
  • *Balrog of Moria* → Lórien; Moria.
  • Permissive "OR" Clauses Omitted: Events featuring an open global mode alongside an optional hold permission (such as *Dragon's Desolation*) leave Site Requirement [Blank] to preserve open drafting.
Target Species
Custom Category		 Biological minion races (Orc, Troll, Undead, Wolf, Animal, Spider, Dragon, Nazgûl, Man), targeted character races (Elf, Hobbit, Wizard, Dúnadan), or species immunity predicates (e.g. Race!=Hobbit && Race!=Wizard).
  • Minion Buffs: Assigned when an event escalates combat for specific minion creature attacks (*Minions Stir*, *Plague of Wights*, *Wake of War*, *Fell Winter*, *Fury of the Iron Crown*, *Words of Power and Terror*).
  • Character Targets: Assigned when an event targets specific character species (*The Burden of Time*, *Call of the Sea*, *Tookish Blood*, *Lure of Creation*, *Night*).
  • Immunity Predicates: Accurately indexes printed character exclusions on corruption hazards (*Despair of the Heart*, *Lure of Expedience*, *Lure of Nature*, *Lure of Power*, *Greed*).
Target Item
Custom Category		 Palantír, Ring
  • Assigned strictly when an event specifically targets, punishes, or triggers off an item classification.
  • Palantír → *Bane of the Ithil-stone*.
  • Ring → *The Nazgûl are Abroad*, *The Precious*, *The Ring's Betrayal*, and *The Will of the Ring*.
  • Enables procedural scenario and solo rules to draft targeted anti-Ring or anti-Palantír hazard packages.
