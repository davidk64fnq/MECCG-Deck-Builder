MECCG Deck Builder

MECCG Deck Builder is a dedicated Windows desktop application designed to build, organize, filter, and export decks for the Middle-earth Collectible Card Game (MECCG) published by Iron Crown Enterprises (I.C.E.), including official expansions and player council Dreamcard sets.

Key Capabilities

  • Complete Offline Catalog: Launches instantly using a local, validated database of official cards and sets with zero startup latency or network dependency.
  • On-Demand Image Preview & Caching: Displays high-resolution card artwork downloaded on demand and cached locally to conserve bandwidth.
  • Five-Section Deck Partitioning: Organizes cards into standard MECCG deck partitions: Pool, Resources, Hazards, Sideboard, and Sites.
  • Live Card Taxonomy Inspector: Inspect official rules statistics and user taxonomy live in the right pane with column-aligned wrapping, filter synchronization, checkmark status tracking, and a pinned quick-assign toolbar.
  • Dual-Layer Filtering: Filter the card catalog simultaneously by official card attributes (race, skills, items, alignments) and your own customizable user tags.
  • Automated Deck Generator: Build complete, tournament-legal decks from customizable rule templates—featuring natural card variety, branching company themes, dynamic leader recruitment, and automatic backups.
  • Multi-Platform Exporting: Directly export decks to Tabletop Simulator (TTS), Play MECCG online client, Cardnum list format, structured text archives, or plain text lists.
  • Custom Taxonomy & Spreadsheet Tools: Scan card text for traits and abilities, link character homes to geographic regions, and audit or edit custom tags in bulk using Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.

Quick Start Guide

  1. Select Card Sets: Click Catalog in the top menu bar to check the expansions you want to browse (defaults to The Wizards).
  2. Filter Cards: Use the bottom panel dropdowns to narrow the catalog by official attributes (such as Attribute: Primary = Character, Attribute: Race = Dúnadan) or your own custom tags.
  3. Add Cards to Your Deck:
    • Double-click any card in the Master List to add it to the active tab on the right.
    • Or drag and drop cards directly into any deck tab.
    • Or right-click a card in the Master List and choose Copy > [Target Tab].
  4. Review Title Bar Counters: Watch the window title update dynamically with card counts: MECCG Deck Builder - "DeckName" (Pool/Resources[Characters]/Hazards/Sideboard/Sites).
  5. Save or Export: Choose Deck > Save Deck (Ctrl+S) to keep your deck in native JSON format, or Deck > Export Deck... (Ctrl+E) to generate files for Tabletop Simulator or Play MECCG.
